### Act II: The Architecture (0:35 – 1:10)

**Visual:**
- Elegant motion graphic diagram illustrating the Doctus architecture:
  1. Google Cloud Veo & Imagen generating assets with signed C2PA extension assertions.
  2. The **Policy Compiler**: intersecting scopes (channels, territories, windows) across the ingredient DAG.
  3. The **Clearance Gate**: single microsecond lookup (< 5ms SLA), fail-closed, deterministic code (never an LLM judgment).
  4. The **Negotiation Loop**: ODRL drafting agent with a human countersignature seam.
  5. The **ClickHouse Rights Mirror**: real-time analytics across millions of assets.

**Voiceover:**
> "Introducing **Doctus**: Chain-of-title as executable policy.
> Doctus elevates C2PA content credentials from passive labels into active authorization code.
> When Google Cloud's Veo generates a shot, Doctus embeds W3C ODRL rights manifests signed under our root PKI.
> As assets are composited, our policy compiler computes the mathematical intersection of every ancestor's rights.
> The LLM can propose actions; only valid signed credentials authorize them.
> The gate is deterministic code that responds in under two milliseconds—fail-closed by construction.
> And the entire rights graph streams live into ClickHouse for studio-scale analytics."

### Act III: The 8-Beat Demo (1:10 – 2:20)

**Visual:**
- Split-screen: Terminal running `uv run python scripts/demo_arc_e2e.py` on the left; asset timeline and gate visualizer on the right.

**Beat 1 (1:10 - 1:20): Veo Generation & C2PA Signing**
- Terminal shows: `[C2PA] Manifest generated & signed under Doctus demo PKI.`
- Voiceover:
  > "Beat 1: Our production agent invokes Google Cloud Veo to generate a hero shot. A signed C2PA manifest with model identity and SynthID verification is ingested into the rights graph."

**Beat 2 & 3 (1:20 - 1:35): Likeness Clearance & Composition**
- Screen highlights talent frame likeness clearance (ALLOWED).
- Next, hero shot is composited with a music bed licensed for festival use only.
- Voiceover:
  > "Beat 2: Talent likeness consent clears for social. In Beat 3, the agent composites our hero shot with a music bed licensed exclusively for film festivals. Native C2PA ingredient edges link them."

**Beat 4 & 5 (1:35 - 1:55): The Block (Gate in Action)**
- Terminal shows:
  - Festival publish: `ALLOWED [Decision #2]`
  - Trailer publish: `DENIED [Decision #3] Reason: SCOPE_EXCEEDED`
  - `Detail: channel 'trailer' outside ['festival']`
  - `Fix: extend scope of ast_music_bed_v1`
- Voiceover:
  > "Beat 4: We publish to a festival channel—it clears instantly.
  > But in Beat 5, the agent attempts to publish a public promotional trailer.
  > The gate blocks it instantly! No hallucination, no LLM bypass.
  > The gate explains why: ingredient ast_music_bed_v1 lacks a trailer license."

**Beat 6 & 7 (1:55 - 2:10): Negotiation & Human Countersign**
- Terminal displays ODRL draft instrument generated by Negotiator agent.
- Human Studio Legal Lead reviews terms and signs.
- Voiceover:
  > "Beat 6: The negotiation agent reads the gate's hint and drafts an ODRL 2.2 license extension.
  > Crucially: the agent CANNOT self-sign.
  > In Beat 7, the human studio lead reviews the terms and countersigns. The new claim enters the graph and permissions recompile."

**Beat 8 (2:10 - 2:20): Clearance Passed!**
- Terminal shows: `Beat 8: Re-publish trailer ==> ALLOWED [Decision #4]`
- Voiceover:
  > "Beat 8: The agent retries publishing the trailer. The gate re-evaluates the updated graph—and clears the release. The loop is closed."
